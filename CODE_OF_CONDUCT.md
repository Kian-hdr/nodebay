# Nodebay code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, patient, and constructive. Welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Respect privacy and do not pressure anyone to disclose personal data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in Nodebay repositories, issue trackers, review discussions, and project spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive conduct reports in public issues.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider impact, context, recurrence, and community safety.

This project also respects the conduct policies of the upstream Boring Notch project and third-party communities when participating in their spaces.
